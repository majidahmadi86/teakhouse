Location

Getting here

Charoenkrung 44, Bang Rak. On the river, in the middle of old Bangkok, six minutes from the BTS.

By skytrain

BTS Saphan Taksin, exit 2. Walk north along Charoenkrung for six minutes; the brass leaf sign marks our lane.

By river boat

Chao Phraya Express to Oriental Pier (N1), then three minutes on foot. The prettiest way to arrive.

From the airport

Private sedan from Suvarnabhumi ฿1,200 or Don Mueang ฿1,000, any hour. Send your flight number after booking and the driver waits with your name.

The neighborhood

Old shophouse cafes, the flower market at dawn, galleries in converted warehouses, and some of the best street food in Bangkok, all on foot.
